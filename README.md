# ESG-Report-Rio-tinto-
## Key Learnings
- Learned how to evaluate a company's ESG performance using a structured ESG scorecard covering environmental, social, and governance factors.
- Understood how to analyse sustainability disclosures, greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1, 2, and 3), renewable energy usage, and decarbonisation targets.
- Learned to identify ESG risks and governance gaps by studying the Juukan Gorge controversy.
- Developed skills in sustainability research, report analysis, and evidence-based ESG assessment.

## Data Sources and Frameworks
- **Rio Tinto Annual Report 2024**
- **Rio Tinto 2025 Climate Action Plan**
- **Emissions Calculation and Climate Methodology 2024**
- **Juukan Gorge Parliamentary Inquiry Report (2020)**
- ESG reporting frameworks referenced in the company's disclosures: **GRI, TCFD, CDP, SASB/ISSB, and UN Global Compact (UNGC)**.

The analysis used publicly available company reports and disclosures to assess Rio Tinto's sustainability performance and identify its key ESG strengths and challenges.
